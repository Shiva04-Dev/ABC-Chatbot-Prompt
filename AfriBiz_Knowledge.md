## 7. Social Impact Initiatives

Afribiz Connect operates two related social-impact ventures focused on township ("Kasi") economic development:

### Kasi Digital Connect
A township enterprise development initiative aiming to onboard approximately 200 township businesses per year, bridging the digital divide by giving township-based small businesses access to digital tools, training, and market visibility. Kasi Digital Connect has established partnerships with organisations including Microsoft Africa, Telkom, Shell, and Nedbank.
Contact: info@kasidigitalconnect.co.za | +27 65 332 1150 | Company reg: 2025/680458

### Kasi 360
Kasi 360 is a scalable technology platform designed to unlock the economic potential of South Africa's township economy, giving small businesses and informal traders the digital infrastructure to start, manage, grow, and scale. It integrates:
- A B2B and B2C digital marketplace
- Embedded digital payments (KasiPay)
- Business management tools (invoicing, stock control, sales reporting)
- AI-assisted employment and skills matching
- Micro-learning and entrepreneur development programmes
- Logistics and delivery integration
- Access to funding, compliance support, and business advisory
- Safety functionality (KasiProtect)

Kasi 360 is designed as a zero-rated marketplace concept, targeting South Africa's more than six million SMEs and informal traders who remain digitally excluded, with a platform model built for replication across other African markets. Kasi 360 revenue streams are designed around monthly SaaS subscriptions, transaction-based fintech revenue, enterprise/skills development programmes, marketplace commissions, digital advertising, and strategic data/ecosystem partnerships.

### Verulam Connect
A related local marketplace project delivered by Afribiz Connect (powered by Overflow PLR technologies), connecting the Verulam community with local businesses and services across sectors — from food and retail to trades and hospitality — through a single, user-friendly, safe online platform.
